# 5. Submit and Present

## Before You Start

1. Click **Use this template** to create your own copy of this repository. Make it private.
2. Add the reviewer account named on your assignment as a read-only collaborator.

## Submitting (Day 7)

1. Check every box in [SUCCESS.md](../SUCCESS.md).
2. Fill in `SUBMISSION.md`.
3. On the platform, submit your repository URL, the commit you want scored, and your Loom link.

If anything is missing, the AI check sends it back with a list. Fix it and resubmit.

## The Loom (5 minutes or less)

One engagement of your choice, spoken to a non-technical client:

1. The problem in the client's terms (30 seconds)
2. The rule you had to dig up and why it matters (30 seconds)
3. The mockup working, including that rule being enforced (2 minutes)
4. The schema and the cost to build and run (1 minute)
5. What production would need (30 seconds)

## The Live Review (about 30 minutes)

Your reviewer schedules it after the AI check passes.

- They pick one engagement, not the one in your Loom. You present it in under five minutes.
- They play the client and push back on a cost and a design decision.
- They ask you to walk through your AI pipeline in two minutes.
- They ask questions from the list below.

## Gap-Check Questions

Prepare for all of them.

1. Walk through your cost estimate for any engagement. How does it change at ten times the usage?
2. LabLine: what stops a result reaching a patient before a doctor has reviewed it?
3. ClauseTrack: what stops your AI summary becoming the thing someone relies on, if it is wrong?
4. ClaimGate: could a claim ever be auto-rejected with no human seeing it?
5. ColdChain: how does your simulator decide to report a breach, and how does the liability report trace back to it?
6. PayRun: what happens if someone tries to edit a closed pay period?
7. Pick a time you corrected your coding agent. What was wrong, and what changed in your next spec or your `CLAUDE.md`?
8. Walk me through your AI pipeline. Where does a human check the AI's work, and why there?
9. What is in your `CLAUDE.md`, and what did you deliberately keep out of it?
10. What would need to change before any engagement could handle real users and real money or medical data?
11. Which domain took longest to scope correctly, and why?
12. Given a two-sentence client problem, what is the first thing you do before writing any requirement?

## Practice (optional)

If communication has been flagged, spend ten minutes a day talking through your work with an AI voice mode (ChatGPT Voice Mode or Gemini Live), asking it to coach your clarity, pace, and confidence. Or record yourself presenting Engagement 1, then again after Engagement 4, and compare.

## Deadline

7 working days from the day you create your copy. The platform reminds you and your assigner. A late submission is flagged and kept on record, nothing more. If you will miss it, say so early.

## Portfolio Use

You may show your own mockups and write-ups as your own practice work, with no copy of this brief and no mention of the programme.
